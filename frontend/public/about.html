Our Story

It started with a question:
why is good snacking so rare?

In 2021, our founders — two food obsessives and one stubborn nutritionist — set out to make a chip that doesn’t make you choose between flavor and feeling good after. Three years of test kitchens, 412 flavor trials and one very forgiving family later, Hey Pop was born.

Today we pop our chips at our Maharashtra facility, work with 80+ farmer partners, and ship across 60 Indian cities.
